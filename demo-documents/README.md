# PaperTrail Demo Documents

These fake documents are designed for the hackathon demo. They contain no real
personal information.

Suggested live flow:

1. Upload or paste `01-lease-renewal-notice.txt`.
   Ask: `When does my lease expire?`
   Ask: `Did my rent increase?`

2. Upload or paste `02-credit-card-statement-subscriptions.txt`.
   Ask: `What subscriptions am I paying for?`
   Ask: `Which subscription should I review first?`

3. Upload or paste `03-insurance-appeal-denial.txt`.
   Ask: `What do I need for the insurance appeal?`
   Ask: `What is urgent this week?`

4. Upload or paste `04-hr-open-enrollment-email.txt`.
   Ask: `What benefits tasks do I need to complete?`

5. Upload or paste `05-utility-past-due-notice.txt`.
   Ask: `Which bills could cause a service interruption?`

Consent demo:

- Click `Send approval`.
- Click `Simulate iMessage reply`, or with live Linq reply:
  `approve address rent, deny medical`

Pitch line:

PaperTrail uses Gemini only as a file reader when needed. Krava is the private
AI brain that extracts structure, classifies sensitive facts, answers questions,
and reasons over consent.
